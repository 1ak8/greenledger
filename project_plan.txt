                        C A R B O N T W I N
          Decision engine for industrial decarbonisation

    "Twin" = a model of the plant, used to test decisions before
                      capital is committed.


================================================================================
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

Indian mid-size manufacturers face carbon pressure from two directions.

Their EU buyers now require installation-level, product-wise embedded emissions
data under CBAM. Where verified data is unavailable, EU importers must apply
default values with a markup - raising landed cost and pushing buyers toward
more transparent suppliers.

Domestically, large listed customers are cascading supply-chain disclosure
requirements downstream under BRSR.

These manufacturers have no in-house sustainability function and no tooling.
They cannot determine which green investment delivers a defensible return.
The result: overpriced consultants, or no action at all.


================================================================================
2. SOLUTION
================================================================================

CarbonTwin converts operational data into two decisions:

   1. WHAT IS OUR CARBON POSITION?
      Product-level carbon intensity (kg CO2e per tonne of output),
      not just aggregate emissions.

   2. WHERE SHOULD THE NEXT RUPEE GO?
      A ranked portfolio of interventions by Rupee saved per tonne of CO2
      abated, constrained by budget.


================================================================================
3. HOW IT WORKS - ILLUSTRATIVE CASE
================================================================================

Sharma Steel, Raipur
    400 employees
    Rs 120 Cr revenue
    18,000 t steel/yr
    20% exported to Germany


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - DATA INGESTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Utility bills, fuel invoices, and production records uploaded as PDFs or
images. Each figure is mapped to its source document.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - CARBON BASELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Annual emissions        : 19,800 tCO2e
    Carbon intensity        : 1.10 tCO2e per tonne of steel
    Sector median           : 1.35 tCO2e/t - ahead of the median
    Estimated CBAM exposure : Rs 2.7 Cr/yr at full phase-in (at EUR 70/tCO2)

Output: product-level intensity plus a full evidence trail - every number
traceable to a source document and a published emission factor.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - INTERVENTION LIBRARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    INTERVENTION                CAPEX       ANNUAL SAVINGS   CO2 ABATED   PAYBACK
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Motor VFD retrofits         Rs 12 L     Rs 9 L           85 t         1.3 yrs
    Scrap sourcing upgrade      Rs 40 L     Rs 22 L          210 t        1.8 yrs
    Furnace efficiency upgrade  Rs 1.60 Cr  Rs 68 L          640 t        2.4 yrs
    Solar 500 kWp               Rs 2.40 Cr  Rs 56 L          532 t        4.3 yrs


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - PORTFOLIO OPTIMISER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Budget entered: Rs 2.2 Cr

    SELECTED  : VFD retrofits + Scrap upgrade + Furnace upgrade
                Total CAPEX Rs 2.12 Cr
    RESULT    : 935 tCO2e abated
                Rs 99 L saved annually
                2.1-year payback
    EXCLUDED  : Solar - lowest return per rupee at current tariff

The optimiser runs as a constrained knapsack problem: maximise abatement
subject to budget and payback thresholds. Solar, the intuitive first choice,
is correctly deprioritised.


================================================================================
4. STRATEGIC POSITIONING
================================================================================

TARGET CUSTOMER
    Indian mid-size manufacturers (Rs 50-500 Cr revenue) with export exposure
    or listed buyers. Regulatory pressure reaches them THROUGH THEIR CUSTOMERS,
    not directly - their CFO has budget and urgency, but no sustainability team.

MARKET ENTRY
    Lead with a free product-level carbon report - the artefact their buyer is
    asking for. Convert to a paid simulator, retain through annual tracking.

    Funnel: Free report -> Simulator subscription -> Annual tracking

CREDIBILITY BOUNDARY
    CarbonTwin produces an EVIDENCE TRAIL, not a verification opinion.
    Every figure links to its source document and emission factor.
    Final verification remains with accredited third-party verifiers.

9. SIMPLE EXPLANATION - PLAIN LANGUAGE
================================================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. THE PROBLEM - ONE FACTORY'S STORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ramesh bhai runs a steel factory. Every day they make steel.

Now the world is asking: "How much CO2 do you emit while making steel?"

    - European buyers are asking  -> more CO2 = more tax
    - Large customers are asking  -> report it, or lose the order

Ramesh bhai has no answer. Because:

    1. He does not know how much CO2 he emits
    2. A consultant costs Rs 15-20 lakh
    3. He does not know where to invest - solar? machine? scrap?
    4. Invest in the wrong place = money wasted


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. THE SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    One line: Software that calculates a factory's CO2 and tells them
    where to invest.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. WORKFLOW - THE DOCTOR'S STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 - TAKE THE TEST
    Upload electricity bill + diesel bill + production register.
    (Photos work too.)

STEP 2 - REPORT CARD

    "Annual emissions: 19,800 tonnes CO2.
     Factories like yours average 24,000 tonnes.
     You are already better."

STEP 3 - THREE TREATMENTS

    TREATMENT                   COST        ANNUAL SAVING   PAYBACK
    ------------------------------------------------------------------
    Replace old machine         Rs 1.6 Cr   Rs 68 L         2.4 yrs
    Buy better scrap            Rs 40 L     Rs 22 L         1.8 yrs
    Install solar               Rs 2.4 Cr   Rs 56 L         4.3 yrs

STEP 4 - ENTER BUDGET
    "I have Rs 2.2 Cr."

STEP 5 - BEST PLAN

    "Do these two: machine + scrap
       Cost        : Rs 2 Cr
       Saving      : Rs 90 lakh/year
       CO2 cut     : 850 tonnes
       Payback     : 2.2 years
     Do not install solar yet - higher cost, lower return."

STEP 6 - DOWNLOAD REPORT
    PDF -> send to the customer. Done.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. LIVE SIMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 7 - PLAY WITH IT

    After the report, the owner can experiment - without spending a rupee.
    Like a video game: change something, instantly see what happens.

    SLIDERS:
        Solar          - on/off, how many kW
        Machine        - upgrade yes/no
        Budget         - Rs 50 lakh to Rs 5 Cr
        Production     - 18,000 to 36,000 tonnes
        Power tariff   - Rs 7 to Rs 10 per unit

    Move a slider -> new result instantly. No waiting.

STEP 8 - FUTURE RESULTS

    The software shows the next 5 years, not just today.
    Example: "Best plan" selected (machine + scrap)

    YEAR    CO2 EMITTED   YEARLY SAVING   CUMULATIVE SAVING
    -------------------------------------------------------
    2026    18,865 t      Rs 99 L         Rs 99 L
    2027    18,850 t      Rs 1.04 Cr      Rs 2.03 Cr
    2028    18,835 t      Rs 1.09 Cr      Rs 3.12 Cr
    2029    18,820 t      Rs 1.15 Cr      Rs 4.27 Cr
    2030    18,805 t      Rs 1.20 Cr      Rs 5.47 Cr

    WHAT THIS MEANS
        Invested        : Rs 2.12 Cr
        Saved in 5 yrs  : Rs 5.47 Cr
        CO2 kept falling every year
        Net gain        : Rs 3.35 Cr

    (Savings rise each year because power tariffs rise each year.)

STEP 9 - PLAN A vs PLAN B

    People say "install solar". The software says "wait, look first."

    METRIC              PLAN A - SOLAR    PLAN B - MACHINE + SCRAP
    ------------------------------------------------------------------
    Cost                Rs 2.4 Cr         Rs 2 Cr
    CO2 cut             532 t             850 t
    Yearly saving       Rs 56 L           Rs 90 L
    Payback             4.3 yrs           2.2 yrs
    5-year saving       Rs 3.1 Cr         Rs 5.0 Cr

    Plan B wins. The software worked this out - Ramesh bhai did not guess.

STEP 10 - SOFTWARE TESTS ITSELF

    4 options available. The software checks every combination:

        16 combinations -> result for each -> pick the best

    ANALOGY: Packing a bag. The bag is small, the items are many.
             Put the most valuable item in first.

    HUMAN                SOFTWARE
    ------------------------------------
    3 months of maths    2 seconds
    Guesswork            Full optimisation


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
